The problem

Your team deserves better
than this workflow

Insurance verification is one of the most time-consuming, error-prone tasks in any dental office. The cost of getting it wrong shows up in surprise bills, unhappy patients, and staff burnout.

⏱
Hours lost every week
Staff spend 30–60 minutes per patient on hold, navigating portals, and manually entering data — time better spent on patient care.
❌
Inaccurate estimates
Missed details like waiting periods, downgrade clauses, and missing tooth provisions lead to billing surprises that damage patient trust.
😓
Staff burnout
Repetitive, manual verification is one of the leading sources of front-desk frustration — and one of the easiest to eliminate.

What we verify

Every detail your team
needs — automatically

InstantVerify AI catches the details staff commonly miss — the ones that cause billing disputes and erode patient trust.

💰
Annual Maximum & Deductible
Individual and family deductibles, how much has been met year-to-date, and the annual maximum benefit.
📊
Coverage Percentages
Preventive, basic, and major coverage percentages — exactly what your team needs to build accurate estimates.
⏳
Waiting Periods
Basic and major service waiting periods that are easy to miss and expensive to overlook.
🦷
Missing Tooth Clause
Automatically flags whether teeth missing before coverage started may be excluded from benefits.
🔄
Downgrade Clause
Identifies if the plan pays for a lower-cost alternative — critical for composite vs amalgam decisions.
📅
Crown Frequency & Ortho
Crown replacement frequency limits, orthodontic coverage percentage, and lifetime orthodontic maximums.
Confidence scoring

No guesses.
Ever.

Every field in your daily report carries a confidence score. If InstantVerify AI isn't certain, it says so — clearly. No fabricated values. No silent errors. Your team always knows exactly what to trust and what to verify manually.

  • Low confidence fields are flagged with a clear warning
  • Unverifiable fields are left blank — never guessed
