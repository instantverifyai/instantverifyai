The cost of getting it wrong

Verification is broken.
Patients pay the price.

Insurance verification is one of the most time-consuming, error-prone tasks in a dental practice — and one of the most expensive when it goes wrong. Surprise bills erode trust. Missed waiting periods cost the practice. And every hour staff spend on hold is an hour not spent on patient care.

⏱
Hours lost every week
Staff spend 30–60 minutes per patient on hold and navigating portals — time that should be spent caring for patients in front of them.
❌
Surprise patient bills
Missed waiting periods, downgrade clauses, and missing tooth provisions become billing surprises that damage trust the practice spent years building.
📉
Money left on the table
Patients with expiring annual benefits are rarely flagged in time. Treatment that could be scheduled before December 31 simply isn't.

What you'll know

The details staff miss.
The ones that cost you.

InstantVerify catches the fields that drive case acceptance and prevent billing disputes — the ones that take staff the longest to find and are the easiest to overlook in a hurry.

💰
Annual maximum & deductible
Individual and family deductibles, exactly how much has been met year-to-date, and the remaining benefit available.
📊
Coverage by category
Preventive, basic, major, endodontic, periodontic, and orthodontic percentages — everything needed for an accurate estimate.
⏳
Waiting period status
Not just "is there a waiting period" — how much of it remains for this exact patient on this exact plan.
🦷
Missing tooth clauses
Automatically flags whether teeth missing prior to coverage may be excluded — caught before the patient is in the chair.
🔄
Downgrade clauses
Identifies when a plan pays for a lower-cost alternative — critical for composite vs. amalgam, posterior vs. anterior decisions.
📅
Frequency limits
Crown replacement intervals, exam frequency, prophy limits, ortho lifetime maximums — pulled from real treatment history.
